Your dealer account, company location and wholesale prices are managed securely by Shopify. Sign in first, then shop on our Shopify storefront to receive your assigned dealer prices.

STEP 01 / SIGN IN

Sign in to your dealer account

Use the exact email address attached to your approved Precision ATV company in Shopify. Shopify emails you a one-time sign-in code; you do not need a separate Vercel password.

Sign in securely with Shopify

STEP 02 / SELECT YOUR COMPANY

Confirm your company location

After signing in, select your approved company and location when prompted. Your Bronze, Silver or Gold catalog is applied by Shopify according to your company's assigned dealer market.

STEP 03 / SHOP

Open the catalog after sign-in

Continue to the Shopify product catalog in the same browser. Dealer-only pricing and availability appear only when Shopify recognizes your approved company session. Prices shown in our separate retail shop are not dealer quotes.

Not seeing dealer prices?
