THE POWERTRAIN.

Precision ATV is a Conroe, Texas powersports business focused on engine builds and rebuilds, dyno testing, ECU tuning, and the parts that support those builds.

Experience behind the build.

Chad and Renae Pope built Precision ATV around hands-on powersports experience. Chad brings more than 15 years in the industry, master certifications, and experience as a lead dealership technician.

Our focus is Polaris and Can-Am engine builds and rebuilds, dyno testing, ECU tuning, and supporting engine and performance components.

Why customers call us.

Engine work is easier when the person helping you understands the complete setup. We help customers match engine packages, rebuild components and tuning to the application, then use dyno data when appropriate to verify the result.
